These do not seem like good readings for Thanksgiving Day. But perhaps they are. “Stand erect and raise your heads because your redemption is at hand.” That is a reason to be grateful. God is with us to bring us through even the most difficult moments, and that is a reason to offer true thanksgiving.

When we face disasters and calamities, there are ultimately only two possibilities. Either it all comes to an end and everything goes back to normal, and God brings some good out of it for his people, as he always does. Or it truly is the end, and Christ returns and brings us his kingdom of infinite peace and happiness forever.

So when we face difficult circumstances, we Christians know the outcome is ultimately going to be good, no matter what. Because Christ rose from the dead, death itself has been defeated. Sin has been defeated. The very last chapter of the book has been written, and Christ is victorious.

And so for us, every moment is a moment of thanksgiving. No matter how much it may seem that sin and death have power over us at any given moment, we know that Christ himself has the final word. We have a reason to rejoice and to be thankful because we know some good is coming. It may be a good in the moment, when things go back to the way they were, or it may be the ultimate good of eternal life. Christ was victorious over sin and death. He is victorious, and he will always remain victorious.

Whatever is happening in the world, we have no reason to give in to fear or worry. We respond with thankfulness and hope, knowing that God is with us, that Christ is victorious, and that we have better things to look forward to.

So today, as you spend time with family, there is no reason to obsess endlessly over what you hear in the news, wondering what’s coming and worrying about how bad things look. We know how it ends. The forces of evil will be defeated. God wins. He has already won, and he is preparing us for something great and wonderful. That is all that matters.

And as Christians, our response in times of trouble is to stand erect and raise our heads, because our redemption is at hand. That is something to be truly grateful for. It is something to spend our lives thinking about and meditating on. Our God loves us enough to rescue us, even from the problems we ourselves have created.

So this is truly a day of thanksgiving. Thanksgiving for all that God has done for us and all that he will do for us. And that is a reason to rejoice and be grateful all the days of our lives.
